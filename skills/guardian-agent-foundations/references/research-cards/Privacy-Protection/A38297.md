## Research question
How can a Mamba (state-space-model) architecture reconstruct hyperspectral images (HSIs) from RGB images more accurately and efficiently, overcoming (1) single spatial perception and (2) single-scale feature extraction limitations?

## Problem definition
Spectral Reconstruction (SR) generates HSIs from RGB images to avoid costly hyperspectral capture. CNNs miss long-range dependencies; Transformers capture them but scale poorly with image size; existing Mamba SR methods use single-scale, spatial-only perception, limiting understanding of complex HSI structure and fine detail.

## System or model being studied
M3SR: a multi-scale, multi-perceptual Mamba U-Net. Its core Multi-Perceptual Fusion (MPF) block has three parallel branches — spatial (VSS/SS2D 2D selective scan), frequency (Discrete Wavelet Transform sub-bands processed by VSS then inverse DWT), and spectral (Mamba block over the per-pixel spectral sequence) — fused with learnable weights. MPF blocks are placed in a U-Net encoder-decoder to extract/fuse global, intermediate, and local scale features.

## Datasets and benchmarks
NTIRE2022, NTIRE2020-Clean, NTIRE2020-Realworld, and CAVE (all RGB↔HSI paired datasets, 31 spectral bands, 400–700 nm). Datasets re-split by the authors because original competition test sets are unavailable.

## Evaluation methodology
Quantitative and qualitative comparison against SOTA SR methods (HSCNN+, HRNet, MST++, ESSAformer, GMSR, RepCPSI, etc.) on reconstruction accuracy vs computational cost (parameters, FLOPs).

## Metrics
RMSE, PSNR, Spectral Angle Mapper (SAM), and Mean SSIM (MSSIM). No privacy or security metric.

## Main findings
- M3SR outperforms SOTA SR methods with fewer parameters and lower FLOPs (author claim; PSNR-vs-params-vs-FLOPs trade-off shown in Fig. 1).
- Multi-perceptual (spatial/frequency/spectral) fusion and multi-scale extraction each contribute to accuracy.
