## Research question
How can multimodal video recommendation choose fusion strategies that (a) respect the semantic structure of content
categories rather than a single context-agnostic prior, and (b) optimize the actual ranking objective rather than
reconstruction fidelity, while adapting quickly to new content domains?

## Problem definition
Deterministic fusion (attention/graph) collapses a solution space of plausible fusion strategies to a point estimate,
ignoring epistemic uncertainty from noise/missing modalities. Two stated limitations: context-agnostic priors (a shared
N(0,I) for all video types) and ELBO objective misalignment (reconstruction ≠ ranking). Cold-start / fast-evolving
content adds an adaptation challenge.

## System or model being studied
VBF++, a variational Bayesian multimodal-fusion recommender. It models a latent fusion-strategy variable z per video
via an approximate posterior q(z|x), with visual/audio/text modalities (d=160), a K=8 cluster context-aware mixture
prior, a cross-modal-attention neural encoder, a reconstruction decoder, and a recommendation head.

## Trust assumptions
Standard supervised/offline recommendation setting; assumes clean interaction logs from MovieLens, TikTok, Kuaishou.
No assumption about malicious users, poisoned data, or model extraction is made or tested.

## Attack or failure mechanism
No attack. The paper's "failure mode" being addressed is a modeling misalignment: variational learning favors
reconstruction-optimal rather than recommendation-optimal fusion strategies. Recommendation-Guided Adversarial
Refinement (RAR) maintains a replay buffer B_good of fusion codes with low recommendation loss (below the 10th
percentile) and trains a discriminator to steer the encoder toward that distribution.

## Proposed defense or method
Not a defense. Method components: (1) context-aware structured mixture prior over fusion codes conditioned on video
semantic category (learnable πk(x), cluster params updated by gradient descent, k-means init); (2) RAR adversarial
refinement aligning fusion with ranking via discriminator + replay buffer; (3) domain-adaptive meta-learning (MAML-style
θ_adapted = θ − α∇θ L_support) for few-shot cross-domain adaptation. Objective: L = L_ELBO + λ_rec L_rec + λ_quality
L_adv + λ_sparse L_sparse + λ_smooth L_smooth.

## Datasets and benchmarks
MovieLens (55.5K users, 6.0K items, 1.24M interactions), TikTok (36.7K/76.1K/726K), Kuaishou (10.0K/292K/8.66M), with
per-dataset visual/audio/text feature dimensions.

## Evaluation methodology
Offline recommendation evaluation vs. GNN and variational baselines; ablations of components; analysis of posterior
variance across content types and uncertainty-guided inference-time sampling. No security/robustness/attack evaluation.

## Metrics
Precision@10 (headline: +4.7–8.3% over SOTA), NDCG@10, hit rate. Posterior variance (e.g., music documentaries 0.184
vs 0.080 single-category; uncertainty-guided sampling +3.2% hit rate on high-variance top-20% videos). No
attack-success-rate or robustness metric.

## Main findings
1) Distributional (vs. point-estimate) fusion improves Precision@10 by 4.7–8.3%. 2) Learned fusion is semantically
interpretable (visual for action, acoustic for music, textual for documentary). 3) Higher posterior variance for
content-ambiguous (cross-category) videos, exploitable via uncertainty-guided sampling for +3.2% hit rate.
