## Research question
Can a single instruction-guided framework handle diverse virtual try-on (VTON) tasks — single/multi-garment, model-free, garment reconstruction, multi-view, model-to-model — by bridging the semantic gap between textual instructions and reference images, and overcoming data scarcity for complex tasks?

## Problem definition
Existing instruction-guided VTON encodes text and reference images separately (e.g., CLIP/T5 text encoder), creating a semantic gap that yields low-fidelity, weakly-controllable outputs; public datasets (VITON-HD, DressCode) provide only single-garment pairs, lacking data for complex tasks (multi-garment, model-to-model).

## System or model being studied
UniFit: (1) an MLLM-Guided Semantic Alignment Module (MGSA) using Qwen2-VL + learnable queries and a semantic alignment loss (cosine to frozen ViT visual tokens) to fuse instruction+reference into explicit semantic guidance Tq; (2) a VAE encoder for low-level reference features; (3) a Diffusion Transformer (DiT) backbone conditioned on [Tq; noisy latent; reference tokens], with a Pose Block and a spatial-attention-focusing loss (MSE on cross-attention vs. ground-truth masks); (4) a two-stage progressive training with a self-synthesis pipeline generating pseudo-paired data for advanced tasks (filtered by DreamSim + Qwen2.5-VL consistency check).

## Trust assumptions
Assumes trustworthy pretrained MLLM (Qwen2-VL) and diffusion backbone, clean public datasets, and that self-synthesized data passes perceptual/consistency filters. No adversarial-input, poisoning, watermarking, or provenance assumptions are made.

## Attack or failure mechanism
Not an attack. Failure modes addressed: semantic gap causing poor grounding of visual details (texture, logo), dispersed cross-attention degrading fine details/introducing artifacts, and data scarcity limiting complex task coverage.

## Proposed defense or method
Not a defense. Method = MLLM semantic alignment (MGSA) + spatial attention focusing loss + self-synthesis progressive training. Purely quality/capability oriented.

## Datasets and benchmarks
VITON-HD, DressCode (~59K pairs per foundational task); self-synthesized 10K multi-garment and 30K model-to-model pairs; MVG + IG-Pairs (12K) for multi-view. Instructions generated with Qwen2.5-VL-7B; triplet samples synthesized with Flux.1 Fill. Compared against TryOffDiff, Any2AnyTryon, CatVTON, FitDiT, AnyFit, MV-VTON.

## Evaluation methodology
Quantitative comparison on garment reconstruction and single-garment try-on (VITON-HD) plus multi-view settings; qualitative comparisons; functionality coverage table vs. prior methods. Ablations implied for MGSA and attention-focusing loss (partly in truncated section).

## Metrics
SSIM (higher), LPIPS, DISTS, FID, KID (lower). E.g., garment reconstruction FID 12.58 and single-garment KID 0.702 reported as best in Tables 2-3.

## Main findings
Author claims: UniFit uniquely supports all six VTON tasks and achieves SOTA/competitive fidelity and perceptual quality; MGSA bridges the text-image semantic gap for higher fidelity and controllability; self-synthesis overcomes data scarcity for complex tasks.

## Additional limitations identified during review
_(reviewer synthesis — not author claims)_
- Self-synthesized training data risks distribution drift / self-reinforcing artifacts; filtering relies on the same model family (Qwen2.5-VL) that generates instructions.
- No consideration of misuse: photorealistic model-to-model and person synthesis is a deepfake/identity surface (nonconsensual imagery, garment/brand forgery) with no watermarking, provenance, or consent safeguards discussed.
- No robustness/adversarial evaluation; SSIM on single-garment try-on is not best (0.883 vs FitDiT 0.895), a mild quality caveat.
