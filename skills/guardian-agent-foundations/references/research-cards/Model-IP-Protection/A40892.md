## Research question
How can a watermark be injected into a latent diffusion model's latent space to carry high-capacity payloads while
minimally degrading image quality, by exploiting the latent space's inherent redundancy and perceptual sensitivity?

## Problem definition
Existing in-generation latent watermarking perturbs sensitive latent dimensions uniformly/blindly, destabilizing
structure and creating artifacts. The paper quantifies differentiated redundancy across latent dimensions and uses it
to allocate watermark energy toward tolerant (high-redundancy) regions, targeting ownership verification and
accountability for diffusion-generated images.

## System or model being studied
RAIN, a plug-in for Stable Diffusion v2.1 (latent dim 4x64x64) with: (a) distribution-alignment initialization — a
pretrained VAE encoder as the initial watermark encoder so the watermark distribution matches the latent prior; and
(b) a redundancy-aware adaptive fusion network that computes per-position redundancy R̃ via PCA energy and guides
embedding amplitude. Recovery is done in latent space via a feature-decouple network and a VAE-decoder-based
reconstructor. Payload is a high-capacity RGB image watermark (512x512).

## Trust assumptions
Latent space tolerates ±0.5 full-dimensional shifts with PSNR >35 dB (empirical justification for injectable
redundancy). Low-energy PCA components carry detail and can host watermark payload without harming primary structure.
VAE encode-decode consistency provides reliable watermark recovery in latent space.

## Attack or failure mechanism
Failure addressed: blind latent injection perturbs sensitive dimensions, causing artifacts/detail loss and quality
degradation, and random watermark initialization mis-aligns with the latent prior, slowing convergence.

## Proposed defense or method
Redundancy-aware adaptive watermark fusion (energy-guided amplitude allocation) + distribution-alignment
initialization, jointly trained with MSE+LPIPS image loss and MSE watermark-reconstruction loss; deployed as a
plug-in requiring no base-model parameter modification.

## Datasets and benchmarks
COCO-derived images referenced; base model Stable Diffusion v2.1. Baselines: Stable Signature, AquaLoRA, SleeperMark,
LW, RoSteALS, MarkPlugger (see Table 1). Some experimental-setting text truncated.

## Evaluation methodology
Quantitative image-quality and generation-quality comparison against baselines at varying capacities; watermark
extraction accuracy (ACC) under perturbations; ablations on redundancy guidance and distribution-alignment
initialization at 2k/10k/13k/25k iterations.

## Metrics
PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS, FID (delta vs. original), CLIPScore, watermark extraction ACC; convergence iterations.

## Main findings
- RAIN reaches ~40.6 dB PSNR and ~0.98-0.99 SSIM with a 512x512 RGB image watermark, outperforming binary/RGB
  baselines on perceptual quality (Table 1); LPIPS ~0.014-0.020; near-zero FID/CLIPScore deltas vs. original.
- Redundancy guidance improves imperceptibility and watermark recoverability at 2k/10k iterations (ablation).
- Distribution-alignment initialization raises 2k-iteration watermark PSNR from 14 dB to 35 dB and ACC from 0 to 0.70,
  and cuts required convergence from 25k to 13k iterations.

## Additional limitations identified during review
_(reviewer synthesis)_
- Robustness is reported against generic perturbations, not against a RAIN-aware adaptive removal/overwriting/forgery

  (contrast with A40921).
- High-capacity RGB image watermarking optimizes fidelity; the paper does not quantify the false-attribution
  (collision) rate across many owners/keys.

## Design implications
Provenance watermarking quality improves markedly by respecting where in a latent representation information can be
hidden (redundancy-aware allocation) and by initializing the mark in-distribution — reusable design principles for any
generative-output provenance signal.

## Implementation implications
Plug-in latent watermarking that leaves base weights untouched is attractive for deployment across model versions, but
recovery-in-latent-space adds an encode step and couples verification to the specific VAE.

## Evaluation implications
Ownership-watermark claims should be validated with adaptive removal/forgery attacks and cross-owner collision
analysis, not only imperceptibility metrics.

## Deployment implications
A recovered high-fidelity image watermark is provenance *evidence* feeding an ownership decision; residual risk from
adaptive stripping and from key/collision management must be governed.

## Monitoring and incident implications
Supports post-hoc source-tracing of suspected misuse/unauthorized redistribution; incident use needs robustness
guarantees under the transformations attackers actually apply.

## Applicability boundaries
Scope: in-generation image watermarking for diffusion-model provenance/ownership. Adjacent to agent security (AI-
content provenance / IP protection), not about agent/tool/prompt runtime security. Do not generalize quality numbers
to adversarial robustness or to modalities beyond images.
