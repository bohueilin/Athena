# Privacy-Protection — Partial Synthesis (chunk 1 of, 33 papers)

Scope: papers A39911, A39975, A40033, A40041, A40045, A40047, A40117, A40132, A40206, A40343, A40398, A40534, A40720, A40773, A40818, A40838, A40839, A40852, A40862, A40868, A40870, A40874, A40889, A40896, A40911, A41120, A42113, A42140, A42151, A42229, A42232, A42372, A42453. All are AAAI-26 (2026) papers in the corpus "Privacy-Protection" category. Every number/claim below is attributed to its paper; where a paper is silent this is stated. "Reviewer synthesis" marks cross-paper inference by this analyst rather than a direct paper claim.

## Orientation: what this chunk actually covers
This is a heterogeneous privacy cluster, not an agent-security cluster. Roughly four sub-populations recur (reviewer synthesis):
1. **Machine unlearning / right-to-be-forgotten** (A39911, A40045, A40343, A40398, A40818, A40870, A40896, A41120, and the unlearning-attack A40047).
2. **Formal / cryptographic privacy-preserving ML** — differential privacy and secure computation (A40117, A40132, A40838, A40852, A40862, A40033, A40720, A42229, A42232).
3. **Inference-time / representation & prompt privacy for (M)LLMs** (A40041, A40534, A40773, A40818, A40838, A40206) plus prompt/embedding-leakage attacks (A40839, A42453).
4. **Media/biometric provenance and agent-privacy behavior** (A40868, A42113, A42151, A42453, A40874, A40911, A42372, A42140).
Only a minority are directly about LLM-agent runtime security (A40874 smartphone agents; A42372 multi-agent industrial eval; A40534/A40720/A40911 provider-as-adversary patterns). Several are explicitly tagged by their cards as *adjacent* or *peripheral* to agent security (A39975, A40132, A40862, A40868, A42113, A42151, A42140).

## Dominant threat models
- **Honest-but-curious / semi-honest counterparty** is the single most common model: a server, cloud model provider, or compute host that follows the protocol but tries to infer private inputs, weights, or attributes. Appears in A40033 (two-party PI), A40132 (semi-honest honest-majority MPC), A40852 (non-colluding two-server), A40206 (honest-but-curious VFL server), A40041/A40534/A40720/A40911 (untrusted cloud LLM/MLLM provider), A42229 (honest-but-curious compute host). Explicitly *no malicious/active adversary* is claimed in A40033, A40132, A40852.
- **Untrusted third-party model provider as the adversary** ("provider-as-adversary" / confused-deputy framing): A40534 (third-party LLM sees KG entities), A40720 (provider holds shared steering vector), A40911 (cloud MLLM sees uploaded image), A42232 (untrusted cloud quantum host). Reviewer synthesis: this is the closest recurring analogue to an agent trust-boundary threat model.
- **Worst-case, adversary-agnostic (differential privacy)**: A40117, A40838, A40852, A40862, A40720 rely on formal (ε,δ)- or ε-LDP guarantees that hold "regardless of auxiliary knowledge"; none of these executes an empirical attack to corroborate the bound (see Benchmark limitations).
- **Post-deletion adversary (unlearning-specific)**: an actor who probes or fine-tunes a model *after* a forgetting operation to recover the "forgotten" data. Black-box single-query membership probing (A40047), white-box relearning via fine-tuning ("RTT", A40343), decoding-switching + multi-granularity MIA (A40818), and a standardized taxonomy of such attacks (A41120).
- **Leaked-artifact adversary**: an attacker who intercepts a released model-derived artifact — a soft prompt (A40839), a steering vector (A40720), or a face embedding (A42453) — and inverts/mines it. A42453 demonstrates this under a *black-box* embedding-only model.
- **Non-adversarial / compliance "threat"**: several papers have no attacker at all — the "threat" is legally/ethically mandated deletion (A40045, A40896), source-data non-availability (A39975), or the model's own weak privacy behavior (A40773, A40874). A42140 is a thesis-proposal abstract with no instantiated threat model.

## Major attack families (as evaluated in-chunk)
- **Membership / former-membership inference**: A40047 (stealthy single-query former-membership on unlearned GNNs, learned dual-encoder + contrastive loss, AUC reported "up to ~0.9+" on some settings per the card, exact cells truncated); A40839 (output-free MIA from prompt vectors, PIPRA, reported avg AUC 87.58% vs 77.05% output-dependent baselines, 90.37% acc on Caltech101 — author-reported); A40720/A40818 use MIA as the evaluation probe; A41120 benchmarks MIA against both unlearn-set D_u and retain-set D_r.
- **Model / template inversion & reconstruction**: A42453 (FEM, diffusion+KAN face reconstruction from FR/PPFR embeddings; ASR at FAR=0.01, e.g., IRSE50 FEM-KAN 83.7 vs MAP2V 77.9; strong-evidence, full paper); A41120 (white-box gradient-matching reconstruction from θ vs θ_u, described as the most severe leakage); A39975 uses data-free reconstruction *benignly* (pseudo-source) but the card flags it as inversion-adjacent.
- **Attribute inference**: A40206 (adversarial classifier infers sensitive attributes from VFL embeddings); A42113 (speaker re-identification from anonymized child audio via ECAPA-TDNN + EER).
- **Relearning / knowledge-recovery on unlearned models**: A40343 (RTT fine-tuning restores forgotten knowledge unless erasure reaches knowledge-dense layers); A40398 (adversarial-prompt reproduction of forgotten code); A40818 (alternative decodings ZeroThink/LessThink expose chain-level residue); A41120 (fine-tuning worsens leakage more than quantization).
- **Data / model poisoning + backdoors**: A39911 (backdoor via a feature party, suppressed to class-prior by unlearning); A40889 (IPM/ALIE/scaling model-poisoning against FL marketplace aggregation, tested to 80% malicious DPs).
- **Deepfake / forgery / evasion**: A40868 (AIGC image detection across 20+ unseen generators, mAcc 93.02%/91.56% author-reported); A42151 (deepfake detection + privacy-by-design synthetic biometrics, doctoral-consortium summary aggregating external numbers).
- **Reconstruction from telemetry (DRA)**: A40862 (single-message Bayesian data-reconstruction attack as a first-class LDP design axis).
- **Verifier-gaming / outsourced-inference forgery**: A42232 (untrusted quantum host returns wrong result; ZK proof detects it).

## Major defense families
- **Machine unlearning** (largest family; note: no exact ontology token — tagged here as incident_containment / robust_optimization / rollback). Mechanistic variants: representation misdirection to a fixed random anchor (A39911, and A40870's RMisU step), token-level output-distribution redistribution (A40398 PROD), layer-level knowledge-density + block re-insertion (A40343 KUnBR), influential-neuron-path editing across modalities (A40870 MIP-Editor), inference-time trajectory suppression without weight updates (A40818 STaR), federated LoRA-based targeted unlearning (A40045 Oblivionis), and certified graph unlearning with fairness (A40896 GUIC).
- **Differential privacy**: subspace-restricted DP fine-tuning (A40117 DP-SFT), DP synthetic ICL demonstrations with single-budget composition (A40838), DP steering vectors after structure-aware reduction (A40720 PrivSV, εd²-LDP under Metric-LDP), two-layer adaptive LDP for prompt routing (A40041 PRISM), RNS-based single-message LDP frequency estimation (A40862), and crypto-assisted two-server DP training (A40852, "2PC-zCDP").
- **Secure computation (HE/MPC/ZK)** (no exact token; tagged secure_aggregation / crypto_provenance / trusted_execution as nearest): HE+MPC transformer inference acceleration (A40033 PCFormer, ~1.9× speedup, semi-honest), RMFE-packed Shamir MPC training over Z_2^k (A40132), CKKS-encrypted argumentative explanation (A42229), ZK proofs of quantum inference with parameter hiding (A42232).
- **Selective / data-minimization protection**: adversarial obfuscation of only the sensitive attribute (A40206 NashCoder), entity anonymization/abstraction so raw data never reaches the LLM (A40534 ARoG), redact-then-recover surrogate editing (A40911 SOER), context-aware routing keeping high-sensitivity prompts local (A40041 PRISM), and output-abstraction (scores + failure clusters, never raw trajectories) for confidential agent evaluation (A42372).
- **Inference-time representation steering**: A40773 (Know-Then-Do, mass-mean privacy-direction steering, training-free, transfers same-source LLM→VLM).
- **Detection / provenance**: A40868, A42151 (synthetic-media detectors); A40874 (privacy-awareness benchmark motivating a recognition→localization→severity→confirmation gate); A42372 (sandboxed server-side execution + submission contract + human gating).
- **Verifiable / robust FL evaluation**: A40889 MartDE (encrypted scoring with client-side decryption, commitment-based verifiable selection, norm-normalization before cosine-similarity trust scoring, anytrust non-collusion).

## Strongest / most replicated findings
- **Approximate unlearning leaves an adversarially detectable residue; "deletion" ≠ "unrecoverable."** Independently supported by A40047 (imprint queryable in a single black-box call), A40343 (RTT relearning restores knowledge from unmodified layers), A40818 (chain-level leakage survives answer-level unlearning and alternative decodings), A40870 (best forgetting only ~80–88%, residual remains), and A41120 (the θ↔θ_u discrepancy is itself a leak; existing defenses "generally lack robustness"). This is the chunk's most robust cross-paper conclusion (reviewer synthesis over 5 papers).
- **Model-derived artifacts are identity/membership-bearing secrets, not opaque tokens.** Soft prompts leak training-set membership even with no output access (A40839); FR/PPFR face embeddings — even "protected" or partial — invert to realistic impersonating faces (A42453, strong evidence, defeats DCTDP/HFCF/PartialFace/MinusFace/PolyProtect/MLP-Hash/SlerpFace/Fawkes under test); steering vectors and pseudo-source reconstructions carry recoverable structure (A40720; A39975 as an implicit demonstration).
- **Selective, sensitivity-aware protection preserves utility better than blanket DP/HE/MPC.** A40206, A40041, A40534, A40911 all argue and empirically support (on their own benchmarks) that protecting only the sensitive part reduces the utility cost of privacy ("no free lunch" is reduced, not eliminated — A40206).
- **Formal privacy guarantees are achievable at lower utility cost by restricting noise/computation to task-relevant structure** — subspace (A40117), structure-aware compression before DP (A40720), single-residue RNS reporting (A40862), bounded MPC-friendly activations (A40852). Each reports near-non-private or near-optimal utility (author claims; formal guarantees stated, empirical attacks not run).
- **The privacy–utility trade-off is intrinsic and recurs across every modality** — text (A40041, A40838), embeddings (A40206, A40720), voice (A42113), images (A40911), explanations (A42229), telemetry (A40862). No paper claims to eliminate it.

## Conflicting / tension findings
- **"Thorough forgetting is achievable" (A40343, A40398, A40870) vs. "unlearning reliably leaves exploitable residue" (A40047, A41120).** Not a strict contradiction: the defense papers demonstrate reduced recoverability *under one named attack* (RTT for A40343; adversarial-prompt reproduction for A40398; behavioral forget/retain deltas for A40870), while the attack/benchmark papers show *other* attacks (single-query MIA, gradient reconstruction, deployment-phase reactivation) still succeed. Reviewer synthesis: the field lacks a shared adversary, so "thorough/near-perfect forgetting" and "unlearning is leaky" are both true against different, non-overlapping tests.
- **Whole-model vs. localized editing for unlearning.** A40343 argues gradient-ascent-family methods only touch "cover layers" and must reach knowledge-dense layers; A40870 argues point-wise neuron editing (DEPN/MANU) is *too* local and misses cross-layer/cross-modal flow, requiring path-level editing. Opposite failure diagnoses of prior art, reconciled only within each paper's own method.
- **Cryptographic confidentiality vs. deployability.** A40033/A40852 report large efficiency gains (1.9×; two-orders-of-magnitude training-time reduction, 642.78 s vs 173,960.9 s author-reported) yet remain semi-honest-only; A42229/A42232 are honest proof-of-concepts that *degrade or stay slow at useful scale* (A42229 fidelity collapses at MLP-L, IO unfaithfulness 0.0499→0.4454; A42232 only noise-free 4-qubit simulation). Same family, opposite maturity signals.

## Defense bypasses / residual attack surfaces surfaced in-chunk
- **Output-suppression defenses do not stop embedding-space attacks** — PIPRA remains effective when output-based MIAs fail under output suppression (A40839).
- **Alternative decoding bypasses answer-level unlearning** — ZeroThink/LessThink expose forgotten content (A40818, the motivating negative result).
- **Deployment operations reactivate "forgotten" knowledge** — fine-tuning (and to a lesser extent quantization) after unlearning increases leakage (A41120); RTT fine-tuning restores knowledge (A40343).
- **Cosine-similarity robust aggregation collapses under norm/scaling attacks** — most FL defenses drop below 20% accuracy under large-factor scaling; norm-normalization is credited as the fix (A40889).
- **"Protected" embeddings remain invertible** — every PPFR/template-protection scheme tested in A42453 still yields impersonating reconstructions (ASR reduced but not eliminated; e.g., FEM-KAN 44.5 on GhostFaceNet for MinusFace).
- **Non-adaptive attacker evaluation likely overstates protection** — flagged by the cards for A42113 (only an "ignorant" attacker; a semi-informed/informed attacker would likely reduce measured privacy), A39911 (single fixed anchor may be trivially reversible by an adaptive party), A40206 (surrogate attacker weaker than a real one).

## Benchmark / evaluation limitations (recurring, load-bearing)
- **Formal-guarantee-without-executed-attack is pervasive.** A40117, A40838, A40720, A40852, A40041, A40534, A40911, A39911 argue privacy via (ε,δ)/LDP composition, Metric-LDP, HE assumptions, or "by construction" (no raw data uploaded) but run *no* empirical membership-inference/reconstruction/linkage attack. Multiple cards independently recommend pairing formal bounds with red-team attacks.
- **Non-adaptive adversaries dominate.** Most defenses are evaluated against fixed, defense-unaware attacks; adaptive-attacker evaluation appears only partially (A40818 decoding-aware, A40889 scaling-designed-to-break-cosine, A41120 adaptive elements, A42372 iterative resubmission).
- **Small models / small data.** Cryptographic-ML papers evaluate MNIST-scale FCN/CNN (A40132), 2000-sample setups (A40852), tabular Breast-Cancer MLPs (A42229), 4-qubit MNIST (A42232); unlearning LLM papers use 6.7–8B models (A40343, A40398), single backbones/synthetic benchmarks (A40818 on R-TOFU, one model). Scaling behavior is asserted, not shown.
- **LLM-as-judge / model-generated ground truth.** A40874 privacy labels are partly GPT-4o-generated; A42372 uses a self-acknowledged-subjective LLM judge; A40773 dataset labels rely on GPT-4o/Claude auto-labeling; A40911 ground-truth edits are themselves MLLM-generated. Privacy "correctness" is therefore model-dependent.
- **Standardization gap the chunk itself names.** A41120 is explicitly motivated by the fact that unlearning-attack papers "each appear to beat predecessors under their own tailored conditions," and provides a shared harness (11 datasets × 10 models × 10 unlearning techniques × 21 attacks/defenses, author-stated counts) — but its own per-cell metric definitions are truncated in the extracted text.
- **OCR/truncation caveats.** Many cards flag truncated result tables (A39911, A40045, A40047, A40206, A40852, A40874, A40889, A40911, A41120, A42453 limitations section). Treat specific per-cell numbers as approximate unless the card marks them verbatim.

## Recurring implementation patterns
- **Representation misdirection to a random anchor** for forgetting appears in two independent papers (A39911 collapse-to-c·u; A40870 RMisU to a random unit-sphere target) plus a retain loss to preserve utility.
- **Structure-aware dimensionality reduction *before* adding DP noise**, to escape the noise∝dimension curse (A40117 subspace; A40720 HCC compression; A40862 single-residue reporting).
- **Redact/anonymize-then-reconstruct-locally** so the untrusted model never sees raw data (A40534 abstract-then-answer; A40911 surrogate-then-recover; A40041 sketch-then-refine on the trusted edge).
- **Swap non-crypto-friendly ops for low-degree-polynomial/soft approximations** in HE/MPC pipelines (A42229 ReLU→x²+x, hard→soft k-means; A40852 bounded comparison-only activation; A42232 trig via low-degree polynomials) — with a recurring faithfulness/accuracy penalty.
- **Emit an auditable evidence record per privacy-relevant operation** — unlearning events (A40045, A40870), ε-budget consumption (A40041, A40720, A40838), commitment-based verifiable selection (A40889), per-request certificates (A40896).
- **Two-non-colluding-parties as the trust anchor** (A40852 two-server; A40889 anytrust DA/AS via ISRG Divvi Up) — with the caveat that the whole guarantee collapses under collusion.

## Product / architecture implications (for the Origin/Guardian/Passport stack — reviewer synthesis)
- **Treat prompts, soft prompts, steering vectors, embeddings, and activation artifacts as first-class secrets** with provenance and egress controls, not opaque blobs. A40839 and A42453 are the load-bearing evidence: sharing a tuned prompt or a "protected" embedding can leak membership/identity with no output access. Any credential-broker/artifact-sharing surface should assume these are invertible.
- **A privacy gate belongs in the agent action path.** A40874 shows off-the-shelf MLLM smartphone agents recognize sensitive actions at RA below 60% even with explicit hints (best Gemini 2.0-flash ~67%); the recommended control is an independent recognition→localization→severity→human-confirmation stage before executing high-sensitivity actions (credentials, financial, precise location, permission grants). This maps directly to a PolicyGuard/ActionGuard-style gate with human_approval on sensitive tool calls — "capability is not permission."
- **Provider-as-adversary is a real deployment pattern for agentic RAG/tool use.** A40534, A40720, A40911, A42232 all model an untrusted third-party model/host inside the trust boundary and keep raw data local (data-minimization / capability isolation of private content from the provider). This is the closest in-chunk analogue to a confused-deputy control for agents calling external models.
- **"Delete my data" / agent-memory purge must be treated as risk reduction, not guaranteed erasure**, and must be paired with residual-risk disclosure, post-deletion re-audit (adversarial membership probes), and least-privilege on dual-version (θ, θ_u) weight access (A41120, A40047, A40045, A40896). Deployment-phase re-fine-tuning is a reactivation hazard.
- **Confidential agent evaluation via output-abstraction + sandboxing** (A42372) is a reusable pattern for evaluating untrusted agents on confidential data — expose derived diagnostics, never raw trajectories, run untrusted code server-side behind a strict submission contract with human gating.
- **Formal privacy claims in product copy must be validated against executed attacks** before trust (recurring card recommendation; A42453 is the cautionary example — schemes marketed as "privacy-preserving" invert under test).

## Open problems (stated by papers and/or reviewer synthesis)
- A shared, adaptive-adversary evaluation standard for unlearning that reconciles "thorough forgetting" claims with residual-imprint attacks (implied across A40047/A40343/A40818/A41120).
- Certified/irreversible removal from parameters (all unlearning here is approximate or behavioral; none claims a formal removal proof — A40343/A40398/A40870 explicitly).
- Empirical-attack validation of formal-DP/crypto defenses at LLM scale (A40117, A40838, A40852, A40720).
- Malicious/active-adversary and anti-collusion security for the semi-honest crypto systems (A40033, A40132, A40852, A40889).
- Query-time defenses (liveness / injection detection) for invertible biometric templates, since reconstruction is offline and undetectable server-side (A42453).
- Leakage-quantified privacy metrics for "privacy-by-construction" systems that currently report none (A40534, A40911, A42372, A39975).
- HE/MPC faithfulness and latency at useful model sizes (A42229 fidelity collapse; A42232 toy scale).

## Most load-bearing papers in this chunk (by id)
1. **A41120 (PrivUB)** — the meta-result: a standardized benchmark showing machine unlearning introduces new privacy attack surfaces and that existing defenses are fragile (tool-combination boosts attacks; DL↔LLM transfer; fine-tune > quantize reactivation risk). Reframes the entire unlearning sub-population's claims. Moderate(-to-strong) evidence.
- 2. **A40047 (FMIA)** — concrete, low-assumption demonstration that approximate unlearning leaves a single-query-detectable imprint (black-box, benign-user posture) across three unlearning methods; the sharpest counter-evidence to "deletion = unrecoverable."
- 3. **A42453 (FEM)** — the strongest-evidence paper in the chunk (full paper, realistic black-box threat model, multiple FR/PPFR targets + commercial API, ASR at fixed FAR): "protected" embeddings remain invertible to impersonating identities. Load-bearing for treating embeddings as secrets.
- 4. **A40839 (PIPRA)** — output-free membership inference from prompt vectors; establishes soft prompts as a standalone leakage surface that output-suppression defenses do not close.
- 5. **A40874 (SAPA-Bench)** — the most agent-security-core paper: quantifies that MLLM smartphone agents lack privacy awareness and motivates an explicit privacy/human-approval gate in the action path.
- 6. **A40343 (KUnBR)** — best-articulated *defensive* unlearning result with a real relearning-attack (RTT) robustness axis and joint utility reporting; the strongest "thorough forgetting" claim, useful precisely as the foil to A41120/A40047.

Runners-up worth citing for the DP/crypto sub-population: **A40117 (DP-SFT)** and **A40838 (DP-ICL)** as representative formal-DP defenses; **A40889 (MartDE)** for verifiable/robust FL aggregation; **A40041 (PRISM)** for sensitivity-aware routing.
