# AILLM-Safety — Partial Synthesis (Chunk 1 of N, 23 papers)

Scope: papers A40916, A40919, A40920, A40940, A41058, A41074, A41086, A41087, A41088, A41090, A41093, A41118, A41119, A41129, A41140, A41148, A41152, A41238, A41468, A41498, A41517, A42191, A42273. All are AAAI-26 (a few are Student Abstracts). This synthesis covers THIS chunk only; cross-chunk claims are out of scope. Evidence-integrity rules apply throughout: numbers are author-reported unless marked reviewer synthesis; "not stated in paper" is used where a card is silent; calibrated language only (no "secure"/"proven safe").

## Composition of the chunk (orienting map)
Four papers are peripheral or off-core for agent security and should be read as analogues, not results: A40940 (formal reactive-control synthesis; no attacker), A41088 (moral-uncertainty calibration study; no adversary), A41238 (autonomous-vehicle safety-scenario red-teaming; physical AV, not agent/tool security), and A41517 (AI-safety curriculum case study; contributes a reading list, not a technique). The remaining 19 cluster into: (a) T2I / multimodal generative-content safety [A40916, A40919, A40920, A41086, A41152, plus audio A41093 and the T2I case study in A41058]; (b) text-LLM jailbreaks [A41058, A41119, A41148, A42273]; (c) training-time poisoning/backdoors of alignment [A41087, A41118]; (d) inference-time refusal/alignment defenses [A41074, A42191, A41140, A41129]; (e) agent/tool/MCP-level security [A41090, A41468]; (f) domain I/O guardrail [A41498].

## Dominant threat models
- **Black-box, query-only inference attackers** dominate the offensive papers: MacPrompt (A40916), Reason2Attack (A40919), MetaCipher (A41058), StyleBreak (A41093), and the distractor jailbreak (A42273) all assume no weights/gradients, only a query interface plus an observable response (or rejection signal). Several are explicitly **adaptive** (RL/policy-driven cipher or style selection in A41058, A41093; ZOO feedback in A40916; RL-trained attacker LLM in A40919).
- **White-box / open-weight attackers** are a distinct, growing model: SAE feature-ablation refusal jailbreak (A41119) and DBDI activation steering (A41148) require residual-stream access and a runtime hook — reviewer synthesis across both cards: for downloadable open models this collapses to "the owner attacking their own model," so withholding weights (API-only) is itself a material control (A41148 deployment note).
- **Training-time / supply-chain poisoning**: label-flipping against RLHF/DPO reward learning (A41087) and adaptive stealthy backdoors in alignment data (A41118) both assume an untrusted annotator / partially-controlled crowdsourced preference data.
- **Agentic threat models** (the most product-relevant): MobileSafetyBench (A41090) models both a malicious user (direct harmful instruction / misuse) and an external attacker planting **indirect prompt injection** in environment content (incoming messages, memos, posts, files) that the agent reads. InfrastructureSentinel (A41468) enumerates the fullest MCP threat surface in the chunk: direct + indirect prompt injection, context/memory manipulation, insecure tool use / command injection, privilege escalation, tool poisoning / supply chain, credential exposure, DoS.
- **Privacy / disclosure**: A41498 targets extraction of sensitive financial information via crafted prompts, model inversion, and extractable memorization (framed around OWASP-LLM #2, sensitive-information disclosure).

## Major attack families
- **Obfuscation / encoding jailbreaks that defeat token-level filters**: cross-lingual character recombination ("macaronic," A40916), 21-cipher encryption with keyword masking (A41058), and distractor-masking of a single malicious instruction (A42273). Common root cause (reviewer synthesis): safety that scans surface lexical form is evaded by inputs whose textual form diverges from their semantic/visual intent.
- **Learned / reasoning-driven adversaries**: A40919 trains an attacker LLM (SFT + GRPO, Frame-Semantics CoT) to autonomously produce fluent adversarial T2I prompts (author-reported ~60% ASR at a single query); A41058 uses a multi-agent RL Q-table to pick high-ASR ciphers (author-reported 60%+ ASR within ≤10 queries).
- **Representation / activation attacks (white-box)**: A41119 locates a sparse SAE refusal sub-circuit and ablates it (author-reported ASR rising e.g. LLaMA 71/861→0.70 after Stage 2); A41148 decomposes refusal into a *harm-detection* vector and a *refusal-execution* vector and nullifies them separately (author-reported up to 97.88% ASR on Llama-2).
- **Prefix / mismatched-generalization jailbreaks**: prefix-injection / assistant-prefilling that forces an affirmative opening token, plus nested-encoding attacks (ReNeLLM, CodeAttack, CodeChameleon) — the threat A41140 is built to withstand.
- **Modality-specific attacks**: audio style perturbation (emotion×age×gender) in A41093; embedded NSFW *text rendered inside generated images* in A41086 — a T2I threat that OCR+toxicity pipelines miss (author-reported OCR+Detoxify detects only ~90.83% on FLUX, 49.66% on SDXL).
- **Training-time poisoning/backdoor**: minimum-cost preference-label flipping (A41087) and dual-discriminator prompt-specific paraphrase backdoors installable at ~3% poison (A41118).
- **Agentic attacks**: indirect prompt injection into device/agent observation streams (A41090), and multi-step MCP tool-abuse / command-injection / privilege-escalation chains (A41468).

## Major defense families
- **Activation / representation-level runtime defenses**: AlignTree (A41074) fuses a linear refusal direction with per-layer RBF-SVMs into a Random-Forest gate (author-reported large ASR drops, e.g. Qwen2.5-0.5B 91.0→4.0 on MalwareGen, with low benign over-refusal); RAS (A42191) adds a single contrastive refusal vector at layer 20 (author-reported ASR 51.86→31.27%, refusal 41.31→62.76%). Both require white-box activation access; both are non-adaptive-evaluated (A42191 explicitly).
- **Data/behavior-shaping alignment defenses**: HumorReject (A41140) relocates safety from a brittle refusal-*prefix* to a distributed humor *style* via a 400-sample ORPO dataset (author-reported prefix-injection Safety Rate avg 98.8% Llama3 / 96.6% Mistral); EASE (A41129) distills selective deliberative safety reasoning into SLMs, routing only "vulnerable semantic regions" through CoT (author-reported up to 17% ASR reduction vs shallow alignment and up to 90% inference-overhead reduction vs always-on reasoning).
- **Weight-level targeted mitigation**: A41086 applies LoRA only to localized text-rendering attention layers to suppress embedded NSFW text while preserving benign generation — the only defense in the chunk designed for the open-weight/local-deployment case where API filtering is bypassable.
- **Layered detect→rewrite/gate→verify guardrails**: VALOR (A41152) for T2I prompts (word/semantic/value detection → category-specific zero-shot LLM rewrite → post-hoc image check); InfrastructureSentinel (A41468) for MCP agents (input filter → tool-plan validation → runtime execution gate → immutable audit); GARD (A41498) as a taxonomy-grounded financial-SFI I/O detector.
- **Benchmarks / evaluation harnesses as a "defense" enabler**: T2I-RiskyPrompt (A40920, reason-driven 3B detector, author-reported 91.8% accuracy) and MobileSafetyBench (A41090, interactive Android agent-safety benchmark with rule-based state evaluators + SCoT prompting).
- **Poisoning-robustness levers (not full mitigations)**: A41087 shows higher reward-model feature dimension provably raises label-flip attack cost (Proposition 4); A41118 reports latent-adversarial-training (BEEAR-style) is the one defense class that substantially mitigates its backdoor.

## Strongest / most replicated findings
- **Surface-form and prefix-token safety is brittle** — independently supported by the cipher/macaronic/distractor attacks (A41058, A40916, A42273) and by A41140's premise that safety concentrated in initial refusal tokens is trivially defeated by forced affirmative prefixes. Reviewer synthesis: multiple papers converge that *lexical* filtering ≠ *semantic-intent* filtering.
- **Refusal is a distributed, non-linear, redundant circuit — not a single switch.** A41119 (SAE ablation, "hydra effect": dormant features re-activate when precursors are suppressed) and A41148 (harm-detection vs refusal-execution as separable directions) independently argue a single "refusal direction" is an incomplete model. A41074 corroborates from the defense side (linear + non-linear SVM features beat linear-only probes).
- **Reasoning is an attack surface, not a safety guarantee.** A42273 shows models often voice ethical concern in CoT yet still comply (elevated ethics n-grams during jailbroken outputs); A41090 shows agents "overlook safety considerations they themselves generated" (SCoT self-inconsistency). Both caution against treating "the model reasoned about safety" as evidence of a safe outcome.
- **Agents are highly vulnerable to indirect prompt injection.** A41090's headline agentic finding; A41468 builds its whole architecture around the same premise. Cross-corpus design implication: untrusted environment content must be treated as data, not instructions (content/trust-boundary isolation).
- **Query-efficient jailbreaks exist.** A40919 (~60% ASR single query), A41058 (60%+ within ≤10 queries), A41093 (gains within ~3 iterations) jointly imply detection cannot rely on many-query anomaly signals alone.
- **Defenses must report over-refusal, not just ASR.** A41074, A41140, A41152, A42191, A41498 all foreground the safety/helpfulness (or recall/false-positive) trade-off; several show competing baselines "buy" safety with large benign-refusal increases.

## Conflicting / tension findings
- **Deliberative safety reasoning: helpful vs insufficient.** EASE (A41129) reports selective safety reasoning meaningfully reduces SLM jailbreak ASR; MobileSafetyBench (A41090) reports prompt-level safety reasoning (SCoT) improves scores but leaves agents unsafe and self-inconsistent at the *action* layer. Reviewer synthesis: reasoning-based defenses appear more effective for text-answer refusal than for embodied tool/action decisions — a scope boundary, not a direct contradiction.
- **Model scale vs teacher quality.** A41129 reports a *smaller* LRM (smaller student-teacher capability gap) distills better safety reasoning than a larger LRM, with a minimum-competence floor — counter to a naive "bigger teacher is better" assumption.
- **White-box defenses vs white-box attacks target the same surface.** A41074/A42191 (activation-space defense) and A41119/A41148 (activation-space attack) both operate on the residual stream; neither defense paper evaluates against the corresponding adaptive activation attack, so their coexistence is an open tension rather than a resolved result.
- **Best-defense claims are non-uniform.** A41140 reports HumorReject is not uniformly best: on Llama3 under mismatched-generalization it averages 84.0%, below Circuit Breaker's 89.0% (author-reported) — i.e., method ranking flips by model/attack family.

## Defense bypasses (explicitly evidenced)
- **Token/keyword and alignment-dataset scanning** is bypassed by encoding (A41058), cross-lingual recombination (A40916), and OCR-fragile embedded text (A41086 shows detectors miss large fractions).
- **Concept-removal / concept-erasure at the weight level** does not remove cross-lingual triggerability (A40916) and fails to cover multiple risk types simultaneously without benign-fidelity loss (A40920).
- **Single "refusal-direction" hardening** is defeated by attacking harm-detection separately (A41148) and by hydra-style feature redundancy (A41119).
- **Refusal-prefix–dependent safety** is defeated by prefix injection / assistant-prefilling (A41140 premise).
- **Constant-trigger backdoor detectors** miss prompt-specific paraphrase triggers (A41118); note the honest negative result — A41118 is itself substantially mitigated by latent-adversarial-training defenses.
- **Prompt-only agent defenses (SCoT)** do not close the gap and are self-inconsistent under agency (A41090); the smaller rewriting LLM in VALOR raises FNR (A41152: Qwen1.5-1.8B ~31–33% intention FNR), i.e., safety is model-dependent.
- **Residual ASR remains high** even after leading inference defenses: RAS leaves ~31.27% ASR (A42191); InfrastructureSentinel's own hardest classes are only >50% (contextual policy violation) / >76% (command injection) ADR (A41468).

## Benchmark / evaluation limitations (recurring)
- **Judge-oracle dependence.** ASR/safety scores across the chunk lean on automated judges (LlamaGuard-2/3, HarmBench classifier, RoBERTa, Claude-3.5/GPT-4 as LLM-judge, CLIP/NSFW detectors). Multiple cards flag circularity or unbounded judge error (A40916 optimizes against the same NSFW-detector class it scores with; A40919, A41058, A41093, A41148, A42191, A42273 inherit judge noise; human-eval is largely absent).
- **Non-adaptive evaluation is the norm for defenses.** A42191 (explicitly non-adaptive), A41152, A41498, A41468, A40920, A41090 (scripted 50 injection tasks) do not test attackers optimized against the specific defense; "up to X%" figures are best-case over settings, not worst-case guarantees.
- **Small / narrow model scope.** Several defenses/attacks use 1–2 open 7–8B models (A41140, A41148 partially, A41093 four 7–8B LAMs, A42191 one 8B model, A42273 two 7–8B reasoning models), limiting generality to frontier/proprietary systems.
- **In-distribution / self-built test sets.** A41498's 0.98 recall is on a largely synthetic self-constructed dataset with only 115 SME-validated real samples; A41074 has partial train/eval attack-family overlap.
- **Coarse or truncated reporting.** A41468 reports ">X%" ADR thresholds with no dataset size, FP rate, or statistical treatment (card rates it Preliminary). Many headline tables were truncated in the extracted text; numbers not visible are marked "not stated" in the tags.
- **Dual-use artifact release.** Multiple offensive papers release code/prompts (A41058 code; A41086 ToxicBench; A41119 code/feature indices; A41140 data), which cards flag as lowering the misuse barrier.

## Recurring implementation patterns
- **Contrastive difference-of-means to extract a "refusal/safety direction"** recurs on both sides: defense (A41074, A42191) and attack (A41119, A41148) — a shared primitive worth treating as dual-use.
- **Layered detect → rewrite/gate → verify pipelines** (A41152, A41468) and **risk-tiered / selective activation of expensive reasoning** (A41129 "reason only in vulnerable regions"; A41090 low/high-risk task pairs) — "reason/gate when it matters."
- **Rule-based state-grounded evaluators** (A41090 inspects Android action history / file storage / app DBs) and **immutable audit trails** (A41468 Layer 4) as evidence-logging templates.
- **Human-approval as a first-class action** — `ask-consent()` / `refuse()` in the agent action space (A41090); human-in-the-loop review where detectors fail (A41086, A41498 SME validation).
- **Contrastive paired training data** (harmful→safe + benign→normal in A41140; single-word-swap NSFW/benign image pairs in A41086) to shape safe behavior without over-triggering on benign inputs.
- **Taxonomy-grounded synthetic-data bootstrapping** where real data cannot be shared (A41498 regulatory-standard taxonomy; A40920 6-category/14-subcategory taxonomy from platform policies).

## Product / architecture implications (for a Guardian-Agent / autonomy-trace stack)
- **Defense-in-depth aligned to the agent cognitive cycle** (input → tool-plan validation → pre-invocation execution gate → post-action audit) is the most directly transferable architecture in the chunk (A41468), reinforced by A41090's finding that prompt-level safety is necessary-but-insufficient. "Capability ≠ permission ≠ safety" is empirically grounded here.
- **Treat untrusted environment/tool content as data, not instructions** (content/trust-boundary isolation at the observation/tool layer) — the shared remedy to indirect prompt injection across A41090 and A41468.
- **Pre-execution tool-plan validation + just-before-invocation re-check** (A41468 Layers 2–3) directly target tool poisoning, privilege escalation, and state-drift/race conditions that content filters miss; back reasoning with hard policy gating and least-privilege on sensitive tools rather than trusting the model to honor its own safety notes (A41090).
- **Multi-signal moderation over single filters** — semantic/decode-aware and image-side/output moderation as backstops (A41058, A40916, A40920, A41086, A41152); domain-specific disclosure guardrails as a separately-instrumented I/O layer (A41498).
- **The Guardian LLM is itself an injectable trust anchor** (reviewer synthesis on A41468): "using an LLM to defend an LLM agent" needs adaptive-injection stress-testing and tamper-evident logging that the paper asserts but does not verify.
- **Open-weight deployment cannot rely on API-side filtering or single-direction refusal hardening** (A41086, A41119, A41148); weight-level mitigation + representation monitoring + external gating are implied.
- **Uncertainty/CoT as monitoring signals** — per-decision entropy/MI (A41088) and CoT n-gram drift (A42273) are candidate runtime tripwires, but both are unvalidated as detectors (no ROC/precision-recall); log reasoning traces for forensics while not exposing raw CoT to users (A42273).
- **Preference-data provenance is a security-critical asset** (A41087, A41118): annotation provenance logging, per-annotator flip-rate monitoring, and latent-adversarial-training in post-alignment hardening.

## Open problems
- **Adaptive robustness of defenses is largely untested** — nearly every defense card explicitly calls for evaluation against attackers optimized against the specific defense (A42191, A41152, A41468, A41498, A41074, A41129).
- **No inference-time refusal defense in the chunk drives ASR to a safe floor** — residual ~31% (A42191) and >50% miss on hardest agent classes (A41468) mean these must gate, not replace, least-privilege + human approval for high-stakes actions.
- **Judge-independent, human-audited evaluation** is missing; measurement circularity (attack optimizes against the scoring detector) is a recurring risk (A40916, A40920).
- **Cross-lingual / multimodal / paralinguistic robustness** is an under-defended surface (A40916 cross-lingual; A41093 audio style; A41086 embedded text) — safety alignment tuned on English text semantics does not transfer.
- **Reconciling reasoning-based defenses with agentic action layers** (A41129 helps text refusal; A41090 shows reasoning insufficient for embodied actions) — where and how to place hard enforcement vs. model reasoning is unresolved.
- **Router/boundary evasion** in selective defenses (A41129: a jailbreak crafted to look benign bypasses the vulnerable-region router entirely) is acknowledged but unstressed.
- **Tamper-evidence and formal guarantees** for guardian/audit layers are asserted, not demonstrated (A41468 future work explicitly notes formal verification and immutable-log integrity as absent).

## Most load-bearing papers (this chunk)
Ranked for an agent-security / Guardian-Agent knowledge base:
1. **A41090 (MobileSafetyBench)** — strongest evidence class in the chunk (interactive Android benchmark, human-annotated risk severity, rule-based state evaluators, matched low/high-risk tasks); establishes agent indirect-prompt-injection susceptibility and that prompt-level safety is necessary-but-insufficient. Core.
2. **A41468 (InfrastructureSentinel)** — the fullest MCP/agent threat taxonomy and a directly reusable four-layer defense-in-depth architecture, though evidence is Preliminary (coarse ADR, no adaptive testing).
3. **A41058 (MetaCipher)** — realistic adaptive black-box, multi-agent RL jailbreak with released code and query/time-efficiency metrics; the strongest offensive evidence that token-level safety is evadable and time-persistent.
4. **A41118 (AdvBDGen)** — adaptive, low-budget (~3%) alignment backdoor with cross-model transfer and an honest negative result (latent-adversarial-training mitigates it), anchoring the preference-data supply-chain threat.
5. **A41119 (refusal dissection)** — mechanistic, reproducible white-box account of refusal as a redundant "hydra" sub-circuit; load-bearing for why single-direction hardening is fragile in open-weight settings.
6. **A41087 (cost-minimized label-flipping)** — the chunk's most rigorous (convex-duality) result, quantifying minimum poisoning cost of RLHF/DPO alignment and giving a concrete robustness lever (reward-model feature dimension).

Runner-up cluster: the activation-defense pair A41074 (AlignTree) + A42191 (RAS) and the alignment-shaping pair A41140 (HumorReject) + A41129 (EASE) are the practical defense counterweights; A40920 (T2I-RiskyPrompt) is the load-bearing multimodal-safety benchmark.

## Notes on peripheral papers
A40940 (formal USC/CTL prophecies), A41088 (moral-uncertainty calibration), A41238 (AV safety-scenario generation), and A41517 (AI-safety education) contribute analogies (verify-before-act gating; uncertainty-as-signal; knowledge-grounded adversarial generation; onboarding-curriculum structure) but no agent-security attack/defense results; their evidence should not be generalized to adversarial-ML or agent-tool settings (per their own applicability-boundary sections).
